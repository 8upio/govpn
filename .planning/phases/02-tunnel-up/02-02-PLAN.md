<objective>
Give the client its tunnel: answer its `PUSH_REQUEST` with a byte-exact `PUSH_REPLY`
carrying an address allocated from `Config.Network`, the fixed `AES-256-GCM` cipher and
the reference's own keepalive expansion — until a real, unmodified OpenVPN 2.6.14 client
logs `Initialization Sequence Completed` and configures its tun interface.

**Phase goal (ROADMAP, verbatim):** "The client brings its tunnel interface up with a
pushed IP and cipher, and encrypted IP packets round-trip between the client and the
embedder's `Session`." This plan delivers the first clause.

Purpose: after this plan a developer running the harness sees a real OpenVPN client
reach a fully configured tunnel interface — the single most legible milestone in the
phase. It also fixes the point at which `Config.OnSession` fires, so plan 02-03 can
hand the embedder a Session that is genuinely usable the instant the callback runs.

Output: `push.go` (the PUSH_REQUEST/PUSH_REPLY exchange), `ippool.go` (the Server-scoped
tunnel-IP and peer-id allocator), `Session.AssignedIP()`, and a relocated `OnSession`
callsite.

| T-02-06 | Spoofing | PUSH_REQUEST handling in `runHandshake` | medium | mitigate | The request is honored only on a TLS stream whose peer certificate already verified (Phase 1 gate) and only once per session: a repeated or replayed request re-sends the SAME assigned address and peer-id instead of allocating again.
